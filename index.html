About me

I grew up in Pakistan and moved to Hungary to study Business Informatics at the University of Debrecen. The degree sits between business and IT, and somewhere along the way I realised the part I enjoy most is working with data and getting models to say something useful about it.

Stock price prediction

Part of my BSc thesis in data science and visualisation.

I built a complete machine learning pipeline on historical Alphabet (GOOGL) stock data. It covers loading and cleaning the data, creating features from past prices, then training and comparing three models: Linear Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting.

The interesting part was seeing how differently the models behave on the same data, and learning why a model that looks great on paper can fall apart on a new time period.

  • Tools: Python, Pandas, scikit-learn
  • Type: Thesis

Interactive dashboard

The visual side of my thesis.

Numbers in a table don't tell a story on their own, so I made an interactive HTML dashboard to present the thesis results. It lets you compare the models' predictions against real prices and look at their error metrics side by side.

  • Tools: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, charting library
  • Type: Thesis

Hotel booking system

A team project at university.

Together with classmates I built a desktop application for managing hotel bookings. It has a Tkinter interface for guests and rooms, and stores everything in an SQLite database.

Besides the code, this project taught me how to split work in a team, agree on a database design early, and merge everyone's parts without breaking things.

  • Tools: Python, Tkinter, SQLite
  • Type: Group project

Data analysis in SAS

Two statistical studies in SAS Enterprise Guide.

The first project looked at how students' habits relate to their academic performance. The second explored a lung cancer dataset to find which factors are linked to the disease.

Both involved cleaning the data, running descriptive statistics and tests, and writing up what the results actually mean.

  • Tools: SAS Enterprise Guide
  • Type: Coursework

Shipping database

Oracle SQL and PL/SQL on a shipping schema.

Working in Oracle SQL Developer, I wrote queries and PL/SQL code against a database of ships, ports and voyages: creating tables and views, changing data safely, and building more complex queries with joins and subqueries.

  • Tools: Oracle SQL Developer, SQL, PL/SQL
  • Type: Coursework
